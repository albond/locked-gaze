# What's New

## 1.1.0

- Three modes: Off, Pass-through and Locked Gaze. Pass-through sends your original video without leaving the Locked Gaze camera, so you can turn correction off mid-call.
- Control Center on macOS 26: three controls that work as a switch, and Mode and Camera, a panel for the mode and your source camera.
- A desktop widget with the same three-way switch.
- Preview: your camera and the corrected view side by side before a call.
- Now runs on Apple M2 and newer chips, including MacBook Air.
- A new peach look and a new icon.

## 1.0.1

- More reliable virtual camera activation.
- Better sync between the menu bar and Control Center.
- Automatic camera selection is now clearly marked by default.
