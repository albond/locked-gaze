# What's New

## 1.2.0

- Browser mode, for Macs whose management blocks the Locked Gaze camera: use Locked Gaze in Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex, Jitsi, Whereby, Slack, Discord and Preply in Safari or Chrome; turn on any other site with the Locked Gaze button in the browser toolbar. It appears only on such Macs; hold Option while opening the menu to see it anyway.
- The Safari extension is part of the app: turn on Locked Gaze Camera in Safari Settings › Extensions. Set Up Google Chrome guides you through adding the Chrome extension.
- Video goes from Locked Gaze straight to your browser on this Mac; nothing is sent online. A site receives Locked Gaze Camera only after you allow it your camera.
- When the Locked Gaze camera is blocked, Pass-through and Locked Gaze are turned off in the menu, the panel and the widget, and Set Up Virtual Camera Again tries once more.
- Windows open in the middle of the screen.

## 1.1.0

- Three modes: Off, Pass-through and Locked Gaze. Pass-through sends your original video without leaving the Locked Gaze camera, so you can turn correction off mid-call.
- Control Center on macOS 26: three controls that work as a switch, and Mode and Camera, a panel for the mode and your source camera.
- A desktop widget with the same three-way switch.
- Preview: your camera and the corrected view side by side before a call.
- Now runs on Apple M2 and newer chips, including MacBook Air.
- A new peach look and a new icon.

## 1.0.1

- More reliable virtual camera activation.
- Better sync between the menu bar and Control Center.
- Automatic camera selection is now clearly marked by default.
